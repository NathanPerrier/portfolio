Controls

Use W, A, S, D or the arrow keys to move and the mouse to look around. Click the scene to begin, and press Escape to release mouse control.

Interactions

Some objects in the room are interactable. These are indicated by the white outline when your crosshair is on it. Simply click on the object when the white outline is present to interact with the object.

Audio

This site uses directional audio and sound effects, so headphones are advised. If you wish to mute the music, press the radio on the desk.
